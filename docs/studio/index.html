Access

Everyone signs in the way your company already signs in.

Storage

Petabytes, on the tier they belong on.

Point Studio at S3-compatible object storage — MinIO, Ceph, a rack of your own or a provider you already pay — and let cold material fall through to cheap tiers while the index stays instant. Multi-site installs replicate only what they need.

  • S3-compatible backends
  • Hot / warm / cold tiering
  • Multi-site replication
  • Bring your own bucket

Licensing

Models you are actually allowed to sell with.

Two of MetaLoom's strongest defaults are non-commercial: the InspireFace packs behind face recognition and Ideogram 4.0 behind image generation. Studio ships a cleared bundle — checked, pinned, versioned and named in your agreement — so the legal question is answered before the pipeline runs, not after a client asks.

  • Cleared model bundle
  • Written provenance
  • Pinned versions
  • Indemnity

Where the line is

Two editions. One codebase.

Community is the whole platform and always will be. Studio is the same software with the organisational parts attached.

Who Studio is for

Not everyone needs a vendor. Some people cannot deploy without one.

Post houses under contract

Client footage under NDA, deliverables with clearance requirements, and an auditor who wants to know which model touched the frame.

Broadcast & public archives

Procurement needs a counterparty, retention needs an audit trail, and the material outlives everyone who set the system up.

Teams building on MetaLoom

You are embedding the engine in your own product. OEM terms, a roadmap you can plan against, and fixes that land upstream.
